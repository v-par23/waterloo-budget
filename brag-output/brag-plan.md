# Brag Plan: WaterlooBudget

## What is this app?
WaterlooBudget is a real, working budget guide to Waterloo, Ontario — curated food/housing/coffee/gym/bar/grocery spots, a map, an AI recommender, a budget tracker, and a "Teams" feature for splitting bills with friends — all wrapped in a deliberate thermal-receipt visual identity instead of generic SaaS chrome.

## The angle
This isn't a joke app, so the video doesn't need a punchline — it needs to look like a real, well-made product. The angle: treat it like an honest app-store feature reel. The hook is the visual identity itself (a literal receipt look — dashed dividers, hard drop-shadow cards, monospace type, one orange accent) landing on screen as if it were printed, then a clean tour of three real screens: Spots, Map, and the Teams "split a bill" moment.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The wordmark "WATERLOO / BUDGET" (two-tone: ink + orange, matching the real nav) prints onto a cream receipt background with a single dashed rule underneath, like a receipt header. Tagline snaps in under it: "A receipt for your life in Waterloo."

## Key moments (the middle)
- A real spot card (Mozy's Shawarma — Uptown Waterloo · Middle Eastern — ~$11 — "Huge portions shawarma") appears with its category tag, price, and dashed divider exactly as it renders in the app.
- The map with the custom category markers (the fork+knife+plate food icon we designed) clustered around University of Waterloo, one marker "landing" with a soft drop.
- A "Teams" split-a-bill moment — an expense row ("dinner · $50.00") appearing with two members' shares settling to "SETTLED" tags.

## Outro / punchline
"WaterlooBudget." then "Built for students who check the receipt." Small "Live now" CTA line.

## User flow worth showing
Browse Spots (filter by category) → open the Map and see the pins → log/split spend in Teams. Entry → key action → result: discover a spot, locate it, pay for it with friends.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: an honest feature tour of a real, useful student app — presented with the confidence of a real product, not a gag. Let the receipt aesthetic itself be the "hook" instead of a joke line.
- Interpretation: clean slide/wipe transitions (0.35–0.45s), title-case feature labels, one feature per scene, no aggressive motion, CTA-style outro. Restraint throughout — the visual identity does the work.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f3efe4 (cream)
- Accent: #ff5a1f (orange)
- Text: #1b1a17 (near-black ink)
- Display font: Space Mono (bold)
- Body font: Space Mono (regular)
- Strongest visual element: the "receipt" system — 1.5px ink borders, 5px/5px hard ink drop-shadows on cards, 1px dashed ink dividers between sections, uppercase tracked category tags on a solid ink background, all-monospace type.

## Share copy (draft)
Built WaterlooBudget — a receipt-styled guide to living cheap(er) in Waterloo: spots, a map, an AI recommender, a budget tracker, and split-the-bill for your friend group.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse professional accents
- Music: a light, warm instrumental bed (bundled Hyperframes track, chosen at composition time for a clean/app-store mood — not lo-fi or cinematic)
- Music treatment: fade in under the hook (~0.3s), steady low bed under the feature scenes, gentle fade under the outro logo
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time (custom track selection); target 1-2 strong-cue locks — the spot-card reveal and the map-marker "drop" — within ±0.15s if a suitable cue lands nearby; no beat-grid sequencing needed since there's no rapid list reveal
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — card drop-shadow presence/depth may breathe very slightly with the music's RMS; no glow, no waveform, no pulsing
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft "print/stamp" tick on the wordmark hook, one card-arrival tick on the spot card, one soft "drop" tick on the map marker landing, one light "check" tick on the SETTLED tag
- Audio-coupled moments: wordmark print-in (Scene 1), spot card arrival (Scene 2), marker drop (Scene 3), expense settling (Scene 4)
- Restraint rule: no music that reads as comedic, chaotic, or lo-fi; nothing loud enough to compete with reading the card copy; no more than one SFX per scene

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Receipt hook — 3s
Cream background. "WATERLOO" (ink) / "BUDGET" (orange) wordmark prints in top-left, a single dashed ink rule draws in beneath it left-to-right, then the tagline "A receipt for your life in Waterloo." settles below in smaller ink mono type.
Sequential/interaction: yes — wordmark settles first (~0.5s), then the dashed rule draws left→right (~0.4s), then the tagline fades/slides up last.
Audio intent: quiet, confident opening — a light instrumental swell starting, not a jokey sting.
Audio-coupled idea: soft "print/stamp" tick exactly as the wordmark settles.
Music: fade-in warm bed begins.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Spots feature — 4.5s
A real spot card recreated at full detail: black "FOOD" tag + "~$11" in orange top row, "Mozy's Shawarma" bold title, "Uptown Waterloo · Middle Eastern" meta line, orange "usually busy" dot + label, "Huge portions shawarma" description, dashed divider, then "Log spend" button ghosting in at the bottom. Small caption above the card: "Budget-friendly spots. Curated, not crowdsourced."
Sequential/interaction: yes — the card's rows reveal top-to-bottom in one quick, coherent card-arrival motion (not beat-locked one-by-one; it's one unit arriving), landing with its signature hard drop-shadow.
Audio intent: a small, satisfying "arrival" moment.
Audio-coupled idea: one card-arrival tick timed to the card's landing/settle, not to each row.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Map feature — 4s
Cream-tinted map view with a few of the app's real black circular category markers (including the fork+knife+plate food icon) scattered near a simplified University of Waterloo label. One marker (the food one) drops in last with a soft bounce/settle. Caption: "See it on the map."
Sequential/interaction: yes — background markers are already settled/static; the food marker itself drops in and lands, drawing the eye.
Audio intent: a light, tactile "landing" feeling.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop/tick SFX exactly on the marker's landing frame.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Teams / split-a-bill feature — 4.5s
A Teams expense row: "dinner" / "Paid by Virat · Sep 10" / "$5.00" in orange, with two member rows ("user dummy — $1.67", "dummy user — $1.67") each getting a small "SETTLED" tag that pops in. Caption: "Log spend. Split with your team."
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two SETTLED tags pop in one after the other, close together (not a long sequential list, so no strict beat-grid needed — two items, ~0.3–0.4s apart, each still legible).
Audio intent: a small resolution/payoff feeling — things getting settled.
Audio-coupled idea: one light "check" tick per SETTLED tag landing.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro / CTA — 3s
Cream background. "WATERLOO" / "BUDGET" wordmark returns, centered, full-size. Tagline line below: "Built for students who check the receipt." Small CTA line beneath: "Live now — waterloo-budget.vercel.app".
Sequential/interaction: none — clean static landing, held for readability.
Audio intent: settle and resolve; music fades out under this scene.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the visual land in silence as the bed fades).
Music: fades out across this scene.
Transition mood: — (final scene)

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but restrained / app-store clean
**Audio summary:** A warm, understated instrumental bed fades in under the wordmark hook, stays low and steady through the three feature scenes with four small, tasteful SFX ticks (print, card-arrival, marker-drop, settle-check), and fades out under the closing wordmark — never louder than the copy, never comedic.
